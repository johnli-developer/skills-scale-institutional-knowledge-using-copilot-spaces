# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
The Product Owner maintains a prioritized project backlog, acts as the voice of the customer, and represents stakeholder interests to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain a prioritized project backlog
- Define and refine requirements and user stories
- Review deliverables and accept work
- Provide clear guidance on feature priorities 
- Collaborate closely with Project Manager and Product Manager

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivered by the team
- Ensure requirements are clear and prioritized
- Facilitate communication between stakeholders and the project team

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and sprint planning sessions
- User story acceptance meetings
- Regular check-ins with Project and Product Managers

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager oversees and coordinates software releases, making sure deployment activities are scheduled and communicated clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning, schedules, and logistics
- Verify release readiness (code freeze, release notes, rollback plan)
- Communicate deployment timing and status to all stakeholders
- Collaborate with Developers, QA, and Project Managers for deployment quality

### Goals
- Ensure reliable and smooth releases
- Minimize downtime/risk during deployments
- Enable transparent tracking of release status

### Typical Communication
- Release schedule announcements
- Readiness checklists and go/no-go calls
- Post-release debriefs

---

## Quality Assurance Specialist

### Role Summary
QA Specialists design, implement, and oversee tests to ensure deliverables meet the team’s quality standards, working with both Developers and Product roles.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute test plans and test cases
- Report, track, and verify issues
- Ensure new features meet acceptance and regression criteria
- Collaborate on automation strategies
- Partner with Developers for quality practices throughout SDLC

### Goals
- Minimize defects in releases
- Ensure agreed-upon feature quality
- Drive continuous improvement in test coverage and effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and bug tracking updates
- Participation in planning and post-mortems
- Daily syncs with Developers

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation, driving detailed requirements and stakeholder alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Analyze workflows, propose solutions, and support decision making
- Ensure requirements are feasible and testable
- Communicate requirements to technical and non-technical stakeholders

### Goals
- Achieve shared understanding of requirements
- Support delivery of business value through clear specs
- Reduce rework by clarifying needs early

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and review sessions
- Clarification meetings between business and engineering
- Documentation of as-is/to-be states

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers own the communication and coordination of process or scope changes, ensuring impacts and actions are clear to all parties.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate, communicate, and manage proposed changes
- Coordinate approvals with stakeholders
- Maintain change logs and update documentation
- Educate the project team on new processes or scope

### Goals
- Minimize ambiguity and confusion during changes
- Ensure all changes are reviewed, approved, and documented
- Support timely and clear rollouts

### Typical Communication
- Change control board or stakeholder meetings
- Change announcements and documentation
- FAQ updates and feedback channels
